Envelope variations & palettes in context

Five ways to treat the envelope inside the pin, plus six full palettes shown doing real work — logo, a Fraunces tagline, Inter body, buttons and the complete colour set. Voice stays as locked: Fraunces + Inter, warm editorial.

Step A · envelope treatments

Five envelopes.

All single-colour. Judge the shape here (shown in coral); colour comes next.

1 · SealedFlap-V. Calm and iconic. The current version.
2 · Open flapLid lifted — an invitation opening. More “event”.
3 · Card peekingA card rising from the envelope. Reads “invite”.
4 · Outline pinHollow pin, solid envelope. Lighter, more delicate.
5 · RounderHeavier pin, softer envelope. Warmest, friendliest.

Six colour systems, in use.
